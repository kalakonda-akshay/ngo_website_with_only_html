About us

Learning does not stop when school does

Lantern began with one borrowed classroom and one solar lamp. Children who walked home from school had no quiet place to study and nobody to ask when a sum went wrong.

Today our centres open from 5 pm to 8 pm. Each one is hosted by a village panchayat or a school, led by a trained volunteer, and stocked with a small library, a tablet for offline lessons and a solar lamp.

We teach to the children's own school syllabus, so evening lessons make daytime lessons easier.

Local teachers first

Volunteers come from the same villages as the children and complete 40 hours of training before they teach.

Free, always

No child pays a fee, buys a uniform or brings a notebook they cannot afford.

Girls stay in the room

We track attendance by gender and visit homes when a girl stops coming.

Questions

Before you get in touch

Who can volunteer?

Anyone aged 18 or older who has completed Class 12 and lives within walking distance of a centre. We train you, so teaching experience is not required.

Do children pay anything?

No. Every centre is free. We cover books, stationery, lighting and the volunteer stipend.

How do you choose a village?

We look for a room offered by the panchayat or a school, at least 30 children who want to attend, and a local contact who will keep the register.

Can I visit a centre?

Yes. Write to us two weeks ahead and we will arrange a visit with a cluster coordinator.
